\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper proposes \textsc{Dante}, a fully decentralized framework for robust decentralized personalized learning over hybrid 5G C-V2X vehicular networks that couples dynamic topology control with resource-aware dual-interface collaboration. We formulate vehicular collaboration as a directed network formation game in which every vehicle seeks to maximize a utility that balances personalized model improvement against communication cost, latency, and trust, under per-round resource budgets. By exploiting the complementary properties of PC5 sidelink and 5G Uu communication, \textsc{Dante} allows vehicles to select not only which neighbors to collaborate with, but also when longer-range collaboration is worth its additional cost. Our analysis establishes convergence and per-round complexity guarantees, and our SUMO-based experiments on real-world road scenarios show that \textsc{Dante} preserves personalization, improves collaboration efficiency, and delivers substantial resource savings relative to representative baselines while remaining robust to unreliable peers.
