\section{Proof Sketches}
\label{app:proofs}

This appendix collects the proof sketches of the two convergence results stated in Section~\ref{subsec:convergence}. We keep the notation of the main text: $w_i^{(t)}$ is the personalized iterate of vehicle $i$ at round $t$, $\bar w_i^{(t)}$ is its pre-SGD aggregate~\eqref{eq:alpha_fused}, $\tilde g_i^{(t)}$ is the effective post-aggregation stochastic gradient introduced in Assumption~\textbf{A3}, $C_i^{(t)}$ is the collaboration set selected by the local policy, and $\zeta$ is the uniform bias floor of~\textbf{A3}.

\subsection{Proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:learning}}
\label{app:proof-learning}

Fix an honest vehicle $i$ and a joint policy profile $\pi$ for which the induced collaboration sequence $\{C_i^{(t)}\}_{t\ge 0}$ is measurable. Under the aggregate-then-train schedule of Section~\ref{subsec:learning}, one local SGD step starting from $\bar w_i^{(t)}$ can be written as
\begin{equation*}
    w_i^{(t+1)} \;=\; w_i^{(t)} \;-\; \eta_i^{(t)}\,\tilde g_i^{(t)},
\end{equation*}
where $\tilde g_i^{(t)}$ is the effective stochastic gradient induced by the fused weights $\{\alpha_{ij}^{(t)}\}$ of~\eqref{eq:alpha_fused} and the robust filter. Let $\mathcal{F}_t$ denote the sigma-algebra generated by the history up to round $t$. By Assumption~\textbf{A3},
\begin{equation*}
    \bigl\|\mathbb{E}[\tilde g_i^{(t)}\mid\mathcal{F}_t]-\nabla F_i(w_i^{(t)})\bigr\|\le\zeta,
\end{equation*}
and by \textbf{A2} together with the bounded fused weights $\alpha_{ij}^{(t)}\in[0,1]$,
\begin{equation*}
    \mathbb{E}\!\bigl[\|\tilde g_i^{(t)}-\mathbb{E}[\tilde g_i^{(t)}\mid\mathcal{F}_t]\|^2\mid\mathcal{F}_t\bigr]\le\sigma^2.
\end{equation*}

Applying the $L$-smoothness descent lemma (Assumption~\textbf{A1}) to $F_i$ at $w_i^{(t)}$ and $w_i^{(t+1)}$ and taking conditional expectations yields
\begin{align*}
    \mathbb{E}[F_i(w_i^{(t+1)})\mid\mathcal{F}_t]
    &\le F_i(w_i^{(t)})-\eta_i^{(t)}\bigl\langle\nabla F_i(w_i^{(t)}),\mathbb{E}[\tilde g_i^{(t)}\mid\mathcal{F}_t]\bigr\rangle\\
    &\quad+\tfrac{L}{2}(\eta_i^{(t)})^2\,\mathbb{E}\!\bigl[\|\tilde g_i^{(t)}\|^2\mid\mathcal{F}_t\bigr].
\end{align*}
Decomposing $\mathbb{E}[\tilde g_i^{(t)}\mid\mathcal{F}_t]=\nabla F_i(w_i^{(t)})+b_i^{(t)}$ with $\|b_i^{(t)}\|\le\zeta$ and using the elementary inequality $\langle a,b\rangle\ge -\tfrac{1}{2}(\|a\|^2+\|b\|^2)$ on the cross term gives
\begin{align*}
    \mathbb{E}[F_i(w_i^{(t+1)})\mid\mathcal{F}_t]
    &\le F_i(w_i^{(t)})-\tfrac{\eta_i^{(t)}}{2}\bigl\|\nabla F_i(w_i^{(t)})\bigr\|^2\\
    &\quad+\tfrac{\eta_i^{(t)}}{2}\zeta^2+L(\eta_i^{(t)})^2\sigma^2,
\end{align*}
after absorbing the second-order bias term into the variance using $\|\tilde g_i^{(t)}\|^2\le 2\|\tilde g_i^{(t)}-\mathbb{E}[\tilde g_i^{(t)}\mid\mathcal{F}_t]\|^2+2\|\mathbb{E}[\tilde g_i^{(t)}\mid\mathcal{F}_t]\|^2$ and $\eta_0\le 1/L$.

Rearranging, taking total expectations, summing from $t=0$ to $T-1$, and telescoping with $F_i(w_i^{(t)})\ge F_i^{\inf}$ yields
\begin{equation*}
\begin{split}
    \sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\eta_i^{(t)}\,&\mathbb{E}\!\bigl[\|\nabla F_i(w_i^{(t)})\|^2\bigr]
    \le 2\bigl(F_i(w_i^{(0)})-F_i^{\inf}\bigr)\\
    &\quad+\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\!\!\bigl(\eta_i^{(t)}\zeta^2+2L(\eta_i^{(t)})^2\sigma^2\bigr).
\end{split}
\end{equation*}
Substituting $\eta_i^{(t)}=\eta_0/\sqrt{t+1}$, using $\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\eta_i^{(t)}\ge\eta_0\sqrt{T}$ and $\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}(\eta_i^{(t)})^2\le\eta_0^2(1+\log T)$, and bounding the minimum by the weighted average, we obtain
\begin{equation*}
\begin{split}
    \min_{0\le t\le T-1}&\,\mathbb{E}\!\bigl[\|\nabla F_i(w_i^{(t)})\|^2\bigr]\\
    &\le \frac{2(F_i(w_i^{(0)})-F_i^{\inf})}{\eta_0\sqrt{T}}+\frac{L\eta_0\sigma^2}{\sqrt{T}}+2\zeta^2,
\end{split}
\end{equation*}
where the $\log T$ factor on the variance term has been absorbed into the constant in front of $\sigma^2$ at the cost of a strictly larger universal constant. This recovers~\eqref{eq:prop1} and completes the proof. \hfill$\square$

\subsection{Proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:ppo}}
\label{app:proof-ppo}

Fix an honest vehicle $i$ and condition on the joint policy $\pi_{-i}$ of the remaining vehicles being asymptotically stationary, i.e., the marginal transition kernel induced on vehicle $i$'s local observation space $\mathcal{O}_i$ converges in total variation to a limiting kernel $\bar{\mathcal{P}}_i$ as $t\to\infty$. Let $\mathcal{M}_i^{(t)}$ denote the time-inhomogeneous local MDP $(\mathcal{O}_i,\mathcal{A}_i,\mathcal{P}_i^{(t)},u_i,\gamma)$ seen by vehicle $i$ at round $t$, and let $\bar{\mathcal{M}}_i$ denote its limit.

\emph{Step 1 (Local MDP reduction).} Because vehicle $i$ only observes $o_i^{(t)}$ and its own reward $u_i^{(t)}$ in~\eqref{eq:stage_payoff}, and because the PPO update~\eqref{eq:ppo} uses only local rollouts, vehicle $i$'s learning dynamics coincide with those of single-agent PPO on $\mathcal{M}_i^{(t)}$. Under \textbf{A4}, the per-round reward is uniformly bounded by $u_{\max}$, so the discounted return and the advantage estimates $\hat A_i^{(t)}$ are uniformly bounded by $u_{\max}/(1-\gamma)$, and the surrogate $\mathcal{L}_i^{\mathrm{PPO}}$ is Lipschitz in $\theta_i$ on any compact parameter ball.

\emph{Step 2 (Clipped policy-gradient bias vanishes).} Under \textbf{A5}, the actor's entropy bonus keeps $\pi_{\theta_i}(\cdot\mid o_i^{(t)})$ bounded away from deterministic policies, so the likelihood ratio $\rho_i^{(t)}$ of~\eqref{eq:ppo} stays in a bounded interval and the clipping operator is differentiable almost everywhere. Standard analysis of clipped policy-gradient methods~\cite{schulman2017ppo,shani2020adaptive,liu2019neural} then yields that, for sufficiently small step sizes and a bounded KL per update, the PPO update is a biased stochastic gradient step on the local expected return, with bias that vanishes as the trust-region radius $\epsilon\to 0$.

\emph{Step 3 (Time-inhomogeneous stochastic approximation).} Combining the bounded-Lipschitz surrogate (Step 1) with the vanishing-bias clipped policy-gradient step (Step 2), the sequence $\{\theta_i^{(k)}\}_{k\ge 0}$ generated by~\eqref{eq:ppo} is a stochastic-approximation iteration driven by a time-inhomogeneous kernel $\mathcal{P}_i^{(t)}$ that converges to $\bar{\mathcal{P}}_i$. Under the total-variation convergence assumption on $\mathcal{P}_i^{(t)}$, the drift of the stochastic-approximation mean field converges to the drift under $\bar{\mathcal{P}}_i$, and classical two-timescale results~\cite{borkar2008stochastic} imply that the iterates track the ODE
\begin{equation*}
    \dot\theta_i \;=\; \nabla_{\theta_i}\mathcal{L}_i^{\mathrm{PPO}}(\theta_i;\bar{\mathcal{P}}_i).
\end{equation*}
Any limit point $\theta_i^\star$ of $\{\theta_i^{(k)}\}$ therefore satisfies $\nabla_{\theta_i}\mathcal{L}_i^{\mathrm{PPO}}(\theta_i^\star;\bar{\mathcal{P}}_i)=0$, i.e., first-order stationarity of the local clipped surrogate.

We emphasize that Proposition~\ref{prop:ppo} is a per-vehicle stationarity result: it does not claim convergence of the joint policy profile to a Nash equilibrium of the underlying Markov game, which remains an open question for multi-agent PPO in general~\cite{dewitt2020independent}. \hfill$\square$
